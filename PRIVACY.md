# 心里有你隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 12 日

《心里有你》是一款无需账号、可离线使用的真心话游戏。本应用不收集、上传、出售或与第三方共享个人数据，也不包含广告、分析统计或用户追踪服务。

玩家输入的姓名、自定义题目和偏好设置仅保存在用户自己的设备上。应用不会将这些内容发送到开发者或任何第三方服务器。用户可以通过删除应用或清除应用数据移除这些本地信息。

本应用不要求创建账号，不访问通讯录、照片、位置、麦克风、相机或其他敏感权限，也不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪。

如果未来版本的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策将同步更新，并在适用时取得用户同意。

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请通过项目的公开支持页面联系开发者：
https://github.com/BrookZeng2023/heart-to-heart/issues

## Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Heart to Heart is an offline conversation game that requires no account. The app does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Player names, custom questions, and preferences are stored only on the user's device. They are never transmitted to the developer or any third-party server. Users can remove this local information by deleting the app or clearing its data.

The app does not request access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or other sensitive permissions, and it does not track users across apps or websites.

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated and user consent will be obtained where required.

Questions may be submitted through the public support page:
https://github.com/BrookZeng2023/heart-to-heart/issues
